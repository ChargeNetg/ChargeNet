Solana network

Charge in public. Settle in a stream.

Private chargers become public nodes. Drivers pay USDC for the energy they use, second by second. Node shares let anyone own a piece of that flow.

The gap

The charger is private. The car is not.

  • A driveway charger sits idle most of the day.
  • A driver still hunts a closed network.
  • The host learns what they earned at the end of the month.

How it gets paid today

The invoice arrives after the cable is gone.

01

Session bill

One charge, one invoice, settled later. The money does not move while the energy does.

02

Closed network

The charger only works inside someone else’s app. A private plug never becomes public supply.

03

Rent, not a claim

The host is a location. They do not hold a claim on the flow their charger creates.

04

Capex only

The charger is a cost. Nobody can buy a share of what it streams.
